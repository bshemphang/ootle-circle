Try a circle, right here

This is a simulation of the exact contract rules. No wallet, no network, no real TARI. Create a circle, pay rounds, and watch the pot move. Then use the calculator below to plan a circle of your own size.

You are the creator and member 1. The other members are simulated so you can run a full rotation in a minute.
