01 / HOW IT WORKS

How a circle works

A ROSCA is one of the oldest savings habits in the world. Ootle Circle puts its rules on-chain, so no one has to trust the organiser with the money.

01

Pool

Five members agree on 50 TARI a round. Everyone pays the same, every round, into one pot the contract holds.

5 members x 50 TARI = 250 TARI in the pot, every round. The contract rejects any other amount.

02

Rotate

The turn order is the join order. Asha first, then Boris, Chloe, Dev, then you. No bidding, no lottery, no favour.

Round 1: Asha. Round 2: Boris. Round 3: Chloe. Round 4: Dev. Round 5: you.

03

Payout

When the last member pays, the whole pot goes to the member whose turn it is. After five rounds the circle closes.

Each member pays 250 TARI in total and takes 250 TARI once. Everyone is whole.

What the contract cannot do

It cannot force a member to pay, and it cannot see whether someone meant to pay. If a round stalls because someone stops paying, the only remedy is the creator closing the circle early, which refunds everyone who paid that round. That is the same trust model as a real-world savings circle, and it is stated here on purpose.
