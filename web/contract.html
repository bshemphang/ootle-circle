What is on-chain

  • Circle state: name, contribution, round length, member list, pot vault, current round, status.
  • One fixed contribution per member per round, enforced exactly.
  • Automatic payout to the member whose turn it is, the moment the last member pays.
  • Round windows: late contributions are rejected, so a round cannot drag forever.
  • Early close refunds the current round; cancel closes a circle that never fills.
  • Every payout goes to an account that was verified to exist when the member joined.

Engine tests

23 passed, 0 failed - engine tests against the real Ootle engine, in-process.

  • Full rotation: three members, three rounds, correct payouts, everyone whole at the end. A two-member rotation completes too.
  • Payout only when everyone has paid; pot holds the partial round until then.
  • Wrong amount, double payment, non-member payment, and wrong-resource payment all rejected.
  • Join twice, join a full circle, pay before active, and pay after the round deadline all rejected. Join at the exact fill deadline rejected; pay at the exact round deadline allowed.
  • Early close refunds the current round, including with no payers and after the deadline passes; only the creator can close early. Cancel and close-early rejected in the wrong state.
  • Cancel after the join window; cancel before it is rejected; bad create parameters rejected; public view getters report the correct state.

Key functions, in plain language

Create a circlecreate(name, contribution, round_length, max_members, join_window) -> Circle

Sets the name, the fixed contribution, how long a round lasts, how many members the circle holds, and how long the join window stays open. The creator becomes member 1.

Join a circlejoin(circle, payout_account) -> ()

Joins with the account that will receive your payout. The contract checks the account exists. When the circle fills, round 1 starts.

Pay this roundcontribute(circle, amount) -> ()

Pays exactly the contribution, once per round, inside the round window. Wrong amounts, double payments, and late payments are rejected.

Claim payoutpayout happens automatically when the last member pays

There is no claim call. The moment the last member pays, the whole pot goes to the member whose turn it is.

Close early and cancelclose_early(circle) / cancel(circle)

Only the creator can close early, and it refunds everyone who paid that round. Cancel closes a circle that never filled, after its join window passes.

01 / STRUCTURE

How the contract is structured

One template, three ideas. The state, the rules, and the money are kept apart so each one can be checked on its own.

01

State

The circle holds its name, contribution, round length, member list, pot vault, current round, and status. Nothing else. The state is the whole truth about a circle.

Name, contribution, round length, members, pot, round, status.

02

Rules

Every call is checked before it touches money: exact amount, one payment per round, member only, inside the round window. A failed check rejects the call and moves nothing.

Exact amount. One payment per round. Member only. Inside the window.

03

The vault

Contributions go into the pot vault. The vault only releases the whole pot, only to the member whose turn it is, only when everyone has paid. There is no partial withdrawal and no admin drain.

Whole pot, right member, everyone paid. No partial withdrawal.

04

The close

When every member has taken the pot once, the circle closes. If a round stalls, the creator can close early and refund that round. A circle that never fills can be cancelled after its join window.

Closed after every member takes the pot once. Early close refunds.

What the contract cannot do

It cannot force a member to pay, and it cannot see whether someone meant to pay. If a round stalls because someone stops paying, the only remedy is the creator closing the circle early, which refunds everyone who paid that round. That is the same trust model as a real-world savings circle, and it is stated here on purpose. The contract is also unaudited. It is tested against the real Ootle engine, but that is not an audit. Do not put money you cannot afford to lose into any unaudited contract, including this one.
